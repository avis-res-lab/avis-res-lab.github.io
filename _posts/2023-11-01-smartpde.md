Plant diseases cut yields and threaten food security, and in many places farmers spot them only once the damage is visible across a field.
**SmartPDE** set out to use deep learning not only to *recognize* plant diseases but to model how they spread and to *forecast* outbreaks, so that farmers can act earlier.

**At a glance**

- **Project:** SmartPDE: Deep Learning Based Plant Disease Epidemiology and Forecasting for Smart Agriculture
- **Status:** Completed (November 2023 – October 2024)
- **Funding:** University Grants Commission (UGC), Bangladesh
- **Principal investigator:** Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan
- **Area:** Precision agriculture

## What came out of it

SmartPDE was also the subject of Muhaiminul Islam's MSc thesis (2024).
Closely related work from the same period produced **[PlantCareNet](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13007-025-01366-9)**, published in *Plant Methods* in 2025.
PlantCareNet is an end-to-end diagnostic system: a CNN classifies diseases from leaf images, and the system then gives two kinds of advice, automated suggestions based on the detected symptoms and expert-guided recommendations for prevention and treatment.
