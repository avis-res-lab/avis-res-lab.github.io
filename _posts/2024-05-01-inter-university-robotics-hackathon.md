Between May 2024 and August 2025, Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan was part of the **Inter-University Robotics and Hackathon Competition**, an ICT-funded project bringing robotics students from different universities together.

**At a glance**

- **Status:** Completed (May 2024 – August 2025)
- **Funding:** ICT (Government of Bangladesh)
- **Area:** Robotics education and outreach

## Why it matters

Competitions like this have students design, build, and program real systems under time pressure. It's one of the best ways to learn robotics outside the classroom.
