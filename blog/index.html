Reaching goals from offline data: GTRL and the goal-representation question
September 30, 2026
ongoing reinforcement-learning robot-learning

Can an agent learn to reach any goal, across long horizons, using nothing but a fixed dataset of past experience? That is the question behind our newest line of work in offline goal-conditioned reinforcement learning (GCRL). In September 2026 we released two preprints on it.

PlantPhenoBot: robotics for plant phenotyping
May 07, 2026
ongoing robotics plant-phenomics

PlantPhenoBot is the lab’s newest funded project. It is a robotics project for plant phenotyping, the measurement of plant traits such as growth, architecture, and stress response. The project began in May 2026 under a UGC–DU research grant, in collaboration with Gazipur Agricultural University.

Thinking Like a Botanist: PlantInquiryVQA in Findings of ACL 2026
April 22, 2026
publication vision-language-models plant-phenomics

Our paper Thinking Like a Botanist: Challenging Multimodal Language Models with Intent-Driven Chain-of-Inquiry is published in Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2026. It introduces PlantInquiryVQA, a benchmark that asks whether multimodal AI can reason about a plant the way an expert does, one targeted question at a time.

Flying with one camera: vision-language models for UAV navigation
April 03, 2026
ongoing uav vision-language-models

Most autonomous drones avoid obstacles with expensive sensors, a prebuilt map, or both. Our UAV research asks how far a drone can get with a single monocular camera and a vision-language model (VLM) that reasons about what it sees. The first answer, VLM-Nav, was published in PLOS One in April 2026. A sim-to-real follow-up is now available as a preprint.

2025

Explainable AI for histological cancer detection
October 19, 2025
ongoing medical-ai explainable-ai

Deep learning can now classify microscopic tissue images with high accuracy, but pathologists rarely trust a model that cannot show why it reached a decision. This project builds cancer-detection models that are both accurate and interpretable. It is funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST) and runs in collaboration with Bangladesh Medical University (BMU).

2023

SmartPDE: forecasting plant disease for smart agriculture
November 01, 2023
completed plant-phenomics precision-agriculture

Plant diseases cut yields and threaten food security, and in many places farmers spot them only once the damage is visible across a field. SmartPDE set out to use deep learning not only to recognize plant diseases but to model how they spread and to forecast outbreaks, so that farmers can act earlier.

IHABOT2: a teleoperated hospital assistance robot
July 01, 2023
completed robotics medical-ai

Infectious diseases spread fastest where people are in close contact, and hospitals are among those places. IHABOT2 was a robotics project to develop a teleoperated, intelligent hospital-assistance robot. Its aim was to help fight contagion by reducing how often doctors must be in direct contact with patients.

2022

Deep learning for neurodegenerative disease diagnosis using image memorability
January 25, 2022
completed medical-ai

Neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer’s are hard to detect early, when intervention helps most. This MOST-funded project investigated deep-learning-driven, automated diagnosis of neurodegenerative diseases using image memorability, a measurable property of how well images are remembered.
