Team

AVIS Lab is a group of undergraduate and graduate researchers working with Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan on computer vision, robot learning, and intelligent systems. Students lead their own projects from first idea to published paper. Recent work has appeared in Findings of ACL, PLOS One, Plant Methods, Smart Agricultural Technology, and IEEE Access. Our alumni have gone on to PhD and MSc programs at the University of Kentucky, the University of Georgia, the University of Central Florida, the University at Albany (SUNY), the University of Dayton, UNC Charlotte, and Mississippi State University, and to AI roles in industry.
