Deep learning for neurodegenerative disease diagnosis using image memorability

January 25, 2022
completed medical-ai

Neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer’s are hard to detect early, when intervention helps most. This MOST-funded project investigated deep-learning-driven, automated diagnosis of neurodegenerative diseases using image memorability, a measurable property of how well images are remembered.

At a glance

  • Project: Deep Learning-Driven Automated Diagnosis of Neurodegenerative Diseases Using Image Memorability
  • Status: Completed (January 2022 – January 2023)
  • Funding: Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST), Bangladesh
  • Principal investigator: Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan
  • Area: Medical AI

Where it led

This was one of the lab’s first funded medical-AI projects at the University of Dhaka. The lab’s medical work has since grown into explainable AI for histological cancer detection, EEG-based rehabilitation and brain–computer interfaces, and bone-health assessment from X-rays.
