# Welcome to AVIS Lab

The **Advanced Vision & Intelligent Systems (AVIS)** Research Group is based in the [Department of Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering](https://www.du.ac.bd/body/RME) at the [University of Dhaka](https://www.du.ac.bd/). We work at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and robotics, building AI systems that can see, reason, and act in the real world. We design and deploy AI-driven solutions across a broad spectrum, from UAV-based navigation and robotic visual servoing to precision agriculture and Industry 4.0 automation, with an unwavering commitment to translating fundamental research into real-world impact.

{% capture text %}

We develop intelligent vision-based systems at the intersection of deep learning, robotics, and autonomous platforms. Our work spans UAV navigation, robotic visual servoing, plant phenomics, and IoT-integrated machine vision, with a focus on deploying real-world AI solutions for Industry 4.0 and precision agriculture.

Our work is published at leading venues and indexed on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=jGC1OfgAAAAJ).
