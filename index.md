# Welcome to AVIS Lab

The **Advanced Vision & Intelligent Systems (AVIS) Research Group** at the University of Dhaka pioneers research at the confluence of computer vision, deep learning, and autonomous intelligent systems. We design and deploy AI-driven solutions across a broad spectrum, from UAV-based navigation and robotic visual servoing to precision agriculture and Industry 4.0 automation, with an unwavering commitment to translating fundamental research into real-world impact.
