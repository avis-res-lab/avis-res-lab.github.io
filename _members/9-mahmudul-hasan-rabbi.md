Md Mahmudul Hasan Rabbi is an Undergraduate Student Researcher at **AVIS Lab** and an undergraduate honours student in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the University of Dhaka. He works under the joint supervision of **Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan** and **Prof. Dr. AKM Azad**.

His research focuses on mathematical optimization, continuous-time generative models, reinforcement learning for reasoning, and multimodal foundation models.

He has co-authored two research manuscripts submitted to *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems*: **SPARK: A Stochastic Optimizer for Fast Convergence in Neural Network** and **AGL Loss: Optimizing Binary Classification by Asymmetric Generalized Logarithmic Loss Function with Statistical Validation**.

His technical work includes implementing deep learning systems in PyTorch, reinforcement learning with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), non-neural visual tokenization, diffusion models, and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs).
