Afnan is currently a Research Assistant at the AVIS Lab, University of Dhaka, and a Junior Research Assistant at [ELITE Research Lab LLC, New York, USA](https://elitelab.ai/). He has completed his B.Sc in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Dhaka, working on Deep Learning for Brain MRI reconstruction under Queensland professional lab supervision.

He has published at *IEEE — "IG-CNN: An Information-Geometric Model for Automated Knee Osteoporosis Diagnosis from X-ray Images,"* presented at IEEE ICECTE 2026.

Previously, he worked at the [SIS Lab, University of Dhaka](https://sites.google.com/du.ac.bd/sis-lab/home) on marine and coastal cleaning robotics, and at BCSIR on an IoT-based security surveillance system.
