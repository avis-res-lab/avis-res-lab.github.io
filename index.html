Welcome to the AVIS Lab

The Advanced Vision & Intelligent Systems (AVIS) Lab at the Department of Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering, University of Dhaka, builds AI that can see, reason, and act in the physical world. Led by Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan, we work where computer vision, machine learning, and robotics meet. Our drones navigate with vision-language models. Our agents learn long-horizon behaviour from offline data. Our models diagnose crop and human disease and explain their reasoning. We publish at venues including Findings of ACL, PLOS One, Plant Methods, and IEEE Access, and partner with agricultural and medical institutions across Bangladesh.

Our Research

We study how machines can perceive and act intelligently, from foundations to deployment. Our areas are robot learning, vision-language reasoning, autonomous UAVs, plant phenomics, medical imaging, and intelligent sensing. Each area pairs new methods with a concrete problem worth solving: counting wheat spikes in a field, diagnosing a diseased leaf, explaining a cancer prediction to a pathologist.

Our Projects

Our funded projects are supported by MOST, UGC, and ICT, with partners including Gazipur Agricultural University and Bangladesh Medical University. Running now: PlantPhenoBot, a field-robotics project for plant phenotyping, and explainable AI for cancer pathology. Past projects range from a teleoperated hospital-assistance robot to plant-disease forecasting.
