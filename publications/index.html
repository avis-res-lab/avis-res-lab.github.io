GTRL: Grounding Divide-and-Conquer Value Learning with Temporal Differences
Abdul Monaf Chowdhury, MD Sameer Iqbal Chowdhury, Shifat E Arman, Md Mehedi Hasan
arXiv  ·  27 Sep 2026  ·  arxiv:2609.33259
