Md Ettashamul Haque is a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [University of Central Florida](https://www.ece.ucf.edu/). His research lies at the intersection of *robotics, embedded AI, and intelligent human-machine interaction*.

He earned his **B.Sc.** in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka in 2025, working as a Research Assistant at the **AVIS Lab** under the supervision of Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan. His undergraduate research focused on an intelligent bionic hand for intuitive human-machine interaction. He redesigned the prosthetic hand's mechanical structure to improve joint stability, grip reliability, and actuation efficiency. He also developed an embedded machine-learning framework for real-time gesture recognition from wearable accelerometers. The work produced an **IEEE conference** paper, *Development of an Accelerometer-based Data Acquisition System for Hand Gesture Recognition* (RAAICON 2024). It also produced an **IEEE Access** article, *Design and Evaluation of ADANet: A High-Fidelity Motion Acquisition Framework for Assistive Gesture-Based Interfaces* (2025).

---

**Research interests:** Robotic manipulation, bio-inspired robotics, prosthetics, embedded AI, human-robot interaction, machine learning on edge devices, sensor fusion, and hardware-software co-design. He is particularly interested in robotic systems that tightly integrate mechanical design, electronics, and AI for robust, adaptive real-world autonomy.
