Gobinda Chandra Sarker is a PhD student and Research Assistant at the [University at Albany](https://www.albany.edu/), SUNY, New York, USA. His research focuses on non-stationarity in reinforcement learning and multi-agent systems.

He completed his **MSc** in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka as a Research Assistant at the **AVIS Lab** under the supervision of Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan. His thesis was *Autonomous UAV Navigation in 3D Terrain through Monocular Vision-Based Depth Estimation and Reinforcement Learning*.
