Gobinda Chandra Sarker is currently a Research Assistant and PhD student at the [University at Albany](https://www.albany.edu/), SUNY, NY, USA. His current research focuses on non-stationarity in reinforcement learning and multi-agent systems.

Previously, he completed his **MSc** in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka, and worked as a Research Assistant at the **AVIS** Lab under the supervision of Dr. Mehedi Hasan.
