Rabeya Akter is a Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at North South University. She received her B.Sc. in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka.

She collaborates remotely with MSc students at the AVIS Lab. She co-authored *Counting Through Occlusion: Framework for Open World Amodal Counting* (2025) with Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan.

---

**Research interests:** Multimodal learning, vision-language models, embodied AI, time-series forecasting, and sign language translation.
