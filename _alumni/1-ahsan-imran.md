Ahsan Imran is a PhD student and Research Assistant at the TrustAI Lab, [University of Kentucky](https://www.uky.edu/), Lexington, KY, USA. His research focuses on explainable deep learning models for image and EEG data, with an emphasis on trustworthy, interpretable machine learning for biomedical and sensor-based applications.

Ahsan was the **first MSc graduate** of the AVIS Lab. He completed his MSc in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka under the supervision of Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan. His thesis, *Advanced Intelligent Video Surveillance System*, developed deep learning models for face recognition in video. It led to *FaceEngine: A Tracking-Based Framework for Real-Time Face Recognition in Video Surveillance System* (*SN Computer Science*, 2024).

---

**Research interests:** Explainable deep learning, EEG/MMG sensor signal processing, biomedical machine learning, and machine learning for image and physiological signal data.
