Muhaiminul Islam is a Graduate Research Assistant and first-year PhD student in Biological and Agricultural Engineering at the [University of Georgia](https://engineering.uga.edu/schools/ecam/). He works with Dr. Md Sultan Mahmud in the Precision Crop Protection Lab. His research combines autonomous robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence to build intelligent field-monitoring systems for specialty crop production.

Before joining UGA, Muhaiminul completed both his **bachelor's and master's degrees** in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka. He spent two years as a Research Assistant in the AVIS Lab under Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan. His MSc thesis, *SmartPDE: Deep Learning Based Plant Disease Epidemiology and Forecasting for Smart Agriculture*, was part of a UGC-funded lab project. His work there covered agricultural computer vision: wheat phenotyping, crop monitoring, plant disease detection, and seedling growth-stage classification.

That work led to publications in *Plant Methods* (PlantCareNet, AI-based plant disease recognition with prevention recommendations), *Frontiers in Plant Science* (WheatSpikeNet), and *Smart Agricultural Technology* (cotton seedling monitoring and growth-stage classification).
