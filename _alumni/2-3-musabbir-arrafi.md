Musabbir Ahmed Arrafi is a **PhD** student in Mechanical Engineering at the [University of Dayton](https://udayton.edu/), Ohio, USA, and a Graduate Research Assistant in the [SMALT Lab](https://smalt.dev/index.html). His doctoral research sits at the intersection of intelligent manufacturing, machine learning, and autonomous systems. He focuses on smart, robot-assisted advanced manufacturing, combining computational and experimental methods to build reliable, scalable engineering solutions.

Before his PhD, Musabbir completed a Master's in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Dayton as a Graduate Research Assistant in the DaRC-ARMS Lab. His master's research covered autonomous navigation, learning-based dynamics modeling, model predictive control, sensor fusion, and multi-agent robotic systems.

He earned his **Bachelor's** in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka, working with the **AVIS Lab** during his undergraduate studies. He was a core member of a precision agriculture project funded by an R&D fellowship from the Ministry of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. He also co-authored a study on predicting urban landslides in Bangladesh's hilly regions with hybrid machine learning and CMIP6 climate projections (*Geosystems and Geoenvironment*, 2025).

---

**Research interests:** Robotics, machine learning, control systems, sensor fusion, and intelligent mechanical systems. His long-term goal is to build engineering systems that combine physical understanding with machine learning, turning data, dynamics, control theory, and design into practical tools for next-generation manufacturing and autonomous systems.
