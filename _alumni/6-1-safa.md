Safaeid Hossain Arib is a Machine Learning Engineer at [ACI PLC](https://www.aci-bd.com/), and previously worked as an AI Engineer at Ontik Technology. He received his *B.Sc.* in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka, where he worked on multimodal learning and continuous sign language translation.

He collaborates remotely with the AVIS Lab. He co-authored *Counting Through Occlusion: Framework for Open World Amodal Counting* (2025) with Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan.

---

**Research interests:** Reinforcement learning for LLM post-training, multimodal learning, vision-language reasoning, embodied AI, and intelligent systems.
